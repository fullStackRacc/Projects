Here's a timeline of Linus Torvald's life:

  • 1969 - Born in Helsinki, Finland

  • 1988 - Started attending University of Helsinki

  • 1991 - Publically releases first Linux prototype.

  • 1994 - Version 1.0 of Linux was released.

  • 1996 - Graduated with a Master's Degree in Computer Science from NODES research group.

  • 1996 - Accepted a position at Transmeta.

  • 2003 - Moved to Open Source Development Labs

  • 2004 - Torvalds and his family moved to Dunthorpe, Oregon
